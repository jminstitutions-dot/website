Privacy Policy

How Jubilee Mission Group of Institutions handles information shared through this website.

Information we collect

When you submit the admission inquiry form on our Contact Us page, we receive the details you enter, such as your name, phone number, email address and message. We use this information only to respond to your inquiry and to guide you through the admission process.

How we use and share it

We do not sell or rent your personal information. It is shared only with staff of Jubilee Mission Group of Institutions who handle admissions and student queries, or where required by law.

Third-party services

This website uses services from other providers: the inquiry form is processed by Google Apps Script, our location is shown with Google Maps, videos are played through YouTube, and some photos are hosted on Google. These providers may collect technical information such as your IP address or cookies under their own privacy policies.

Your choices
